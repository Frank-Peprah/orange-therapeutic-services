What we support

Start with what you're noticing

You don't need a diagnosis to contact us. If something about your child's development, learning or behaviour worries you, an assessment is the right first step.

Social & communication

Autism

Communication, play, routines and social connection.

Attention

ADHD & focus

Attention, impulse control and organisation at home and in class.

Language

Speech & language delay

Late talking, unclear speech, or trouble understanding instructions.

Learning

Learning differences

Difficulty with reading, writing, spelling or numbers.

Behaviour

Emotional regulation

Meltdowns, anxiety, aggression, or trouble with transitions.

Milestones

Developmental delay

Sitting, walking, fine motor skills or self-care coming later than expected.

Senses

Sensory processing

Strong reactions to noise, textures, food or crowds.

Wellbeing

Trauma-related needs

Children affected by loss, disruption or difficult experiences.

How it works

From first call to measurable progress

  1. Get in touch

    Call, send a WhatsApp message or fill in the form. We reply within one working day.

  2. Assessment

    We meet your child, talk with you, and where helpful observe them at school.

  3. Personal plan

    Clear goals, the therapies we recommend, and what you can practise at home.

  4. Sessions & reviews

    Regular sessions, plus a written progress report at the end of every term.

Questions

Frequently asked

Does my child need a diagnosis first?

No. Many families come to us with a concern rather than a diagnosis. The assessment helps clarify your child's needs and the next steps.

What ages do you work with?

We work with children aged — to —. Contact us if you're unsure whether we can help.

Do you offer sessions online?

Yes, for some therapies and for parent coaching. Your assessment will show whether online sessions suit your child.

How do term packages work?

You reserve a fixed weekly slot for the whole school term. At the end of the term you receive a written progress report and meet with us to plan the next term.

Can you work with my child's school?

Yes. With your permission we can observe your child at school and share strategies with their teachers so support is consistent.

How do you protect my child's information?

We collect only what we need, store it securely, and never share it without your written consent, except where a child's safety requires it. Read our Privacy Notice and Safeguarding Statement.

Is this an emergency service?

No. If a child is in danger or there is a medical emergency, call 112 or go to the nearest hospital emergency department.
